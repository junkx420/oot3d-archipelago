Update both together.

## New

- Gold Skulltula token shuffle (dungeon, overworld or all). A token counts
  however you take it, also while disconnected; where its location holds
  another item, that item comes from the server.
- Deku scrub shuffle (the three one-time scrubs, or all). A scrub sells the
  location's item once, then its own; prices are the game's.
- "Night Gold Skulltulas expect Sun's Song" (logic only).

Not yet: cows and shops, which need changes in the game's code.

Tested headless with a seed shuffling every token and scrub (408
locations): the dev build and the release exe in a copy of a Forge-prepared
beta.1.2 installation; tokens already in the save were reported on
connecting. Not yet seen in the game: taking a shuffled token or buying from
a shuffled scrub.

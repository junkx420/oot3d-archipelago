## New options

- Rainbow bridge by stones, medallions, dungeon rewards, dungeons or Gold
  Skulltula tokens: the bridge appears once your save has enough.
- Triforce hunt: collect the required Triforce Pieces to win; an in-game
  message counts them.
- Starting hearts (1 to 20) for a new file.
- Shuffle ocarinas, the Weird Egg and the Gerudo membership card.

Tested headless with seeds using the new options: every location is in the
client's table, and the release exe in a copy of a Forge-prepared beta.1.2
installation connects and scouts. Not yet seen in the game.

## New

All in the F1 menu, Game > Archipelago, and off (or x1) by default. They
work with or without a connection and change nothing the seed or the server
sees.

- **Cheats** (as in Ship of Harkinian): infinite health, magic, ammo and
  rupees. Each keeps what Link already has full, up to his capacity: no
  magic before the meter, no arrows before the bow. A hit bigger than all
  hearts and a DeathLink death still kill Link.
- **Fast chests**: every chest opens as a small one does, without the slow
  opening, the light and the fanfare (as the 3D randomizer's "always fast"
  chest animations).
- **Song replays**: *Skip* does not play a song back after Link plays it;
  *Skip, no sound* skips the replay's music too (not for the Scarecrow's
  Song). The Song of Time and warp blocks do not wait for the skipped
  replay. As the 3D randomizer's "Skip Song Replays".
- **Damage taken**: x1/2, x2, x4, x8, x16 or one hit KO, as the 3D
  randomizer's damage multiplier. Healing is not changed; Master Quest keeps
  its double damage. The seed's logic assumes x1.

None of them patches game code: they work through the client's hooks, which
check the game's code before they start.

## Known

- The cheats were seen in the game (full rupees and ammo). Fast chests, the
  song replay skip and the damage multiplier are tested on the host, and
  their hooks were checked against the game's code in a run, but they were
  not yet tried on a chest, a song or a hit in the game.
- With the song replay skip the song's light effects still play out (the
  3D randomizer removes them).

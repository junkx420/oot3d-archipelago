`oot3d.apworld` world version 0.12.0, for Archipelago 0.6.7 or newer. SHA-256

## Fixed

The logic follows OoT3D Randomizer, which changes the game where it tests
what Link owns instead of whether a location was taken. Without those
changes some locations could never be taken in v0.1.0 and the alphas:

- **Items Link already owns from elsewhere:** Darunia's Joy (strength), the
  Lake Hylia sun (Fire Arrows), Link the Goron (Goron Tunic; he also
  ignored Link once he had the Fire Medallion), King Zora (Zora Tunic) and
  Biggoron (Biggoron's Sword) now give their location until it is taken,
  whatever Link owns.
- **The child rolling Goron** talks to Link without a bomb bag, as the logic
  allows bombchus or the bracelet trick to stop him.
- **Sheik in the Ice Cavern** appears once the Iron Boots chest is open, not
  once Link owns the Iron Boots.
- **Upgrades from minigames and NPCs:** the shooting galleries, the Lost
  Woods target, horseback archery, bombchu bowling, the Deku Theater and the
  scrubs' stick and nut upgrades offer the next capacity in the game, so with
  an upgrade received early their location was not found. They are now
  found at any capacity. The Gold Skulltula family's 10 and 30 token rewards
  could report each other's location; now each reports its own.

None of this patches game code: for the one test the game makes, the client
shows it the location's state and then restores the save.

## New

- **Shuffle Songs**: off (default), song locations, dungeon rewards or
  anywhere. A teacher whose location holds something else still plays the
  game's song scene, but Link learns nothing there: the location's item
  comes from the server. Impa, Malon, Saria and the Royal Family's Tomb,
  who in the game teach only a Link who does not know their song, teach
  until their location is taken.

## Known

- The fixed NPCs and the song teachers were not yet played with a seed; the
  fixes follow the game's code and the 3D randomizer's patches, each read in
  the EUR game.
- With shuffled songs the teacher's "You learned ..." text names the game's
  song. Learning a song by playing it back without an ocarina (ocarina
  shuffle) was not tried.
- Not supported yet: dungeon reward shuffle, Master Quest, entrance shuffle,
  models for other games' items. The full list is in `KNOWN_ISSUES.md`.

Chests that match their contents, and song and reward scenes that name
what their location holds. `TriAevum-Archipelago-v0.4.0-Windows-x64.zip`
holds a new `TriAevum.exe` for an installed TriAevum v0.8.0-beta.1.2; see
`README-ARCHIPELAGO.txt` in it. `oot3d.apworld` is unchanged (world version
0.13.0, SHA-256
`fbfe166216e0b283c7d6c5bf2dda7e5440e8eea1605e504013971b808ccf1c0f`): keep
the one from v0.3.0 or take it again from here.

## New

- **Chests: Match contents** (F1 > Archipelago, off by default), as OoT3D
  Randomizer's "Classic" chest appearance: a chest with a progression item,
  a trap or a boss key is big, any other small; a chest with a key has the
  fancy model. For another game's item, "progression" is what Archipelago
  says about it. The randomizer's moves of Ganon's Castle's and the Spirit
  Temple's chests that would be out of reach at the other size are kept.
  It applies to chests that appear after it is turned on, once the client
  is connected.
- With shuffled songs or dungeon rewards, a teacher's "You learned ..." or
  a reward scene's "You got ..." now names the item the location really
  holds ("You found Hookshot for Alice!") instead of the game's song or
  reward, which Link does not get there.

None of this patches game code.

## Checked

- Host tests for every chest size and model and for every song and reward
  text.
- In the game (developer build, a local seed): in Mido's house the four
  chests were changed; the Progressive Magic Meter's chest was big, the
  three with minor items stayed small. With the option off nothing changed.
- In a copy of an installed TriAevum the new `TriAevum.exe` matched all 105
  of its game-code checks.

## Known

- A chest was not yet opened with the option on, and the chests in
  dungeons and the moved chests were not seen.
- The new song and reward texts were not yet seen in the game; the song
  teacher's and reward scene's pictures still show the game's song or
  reward.
- The randomizer's chest textures (gold, key, heart, skull) are not
  available.
- Not supported yet: Master Quest, entrance shuffle, models for other
  games' items. The full list is in `KNOWN_ISSUES.md`.

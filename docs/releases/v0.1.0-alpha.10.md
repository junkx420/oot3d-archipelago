## New

- **Glitched logic** (Options Creator group "Glitches", collapsed by
  default). **Logic Rules: glitched** turns on the OoT3D Randomizer's
  glitched conditions. Its 21 glitch categories (ISG, Bomb Hover, Megaflip,
  QPA, Hookshot Clip, Ledge Clip, ...) each have the difficulties the 3D
  randomizer gives them (Novice to Hero); its 14 single glitches (Blue Fire
  walls, child in the Gerudo Valley tent, adult in Jabu-Jabu, Forest boss key
  skip, ...) are toggles. As in 3D, some glitches raise the small keys a
  door needs. Each category and single glitch was compared with the 3D
  randomizer's own logic: with it on, the logic never expects more than the
  3D randomizer does.
- Universal Tracker rebuilds the seed's glitches from its slot data.

## Fixed

These apply to glitchless seeds too; both make the logic stricter, as in 3D.

- The Bottle with Big Poe no longer counts as a bottle (for example for
  King Zora or the blue fire in the Ice Cavern).
- Sheik in the Ice Cavern now needs adult Link.

## Known

- 38 of the 3D randomizer's 274 glitched conditions are left out, so a
  glitched seed expects less than the 3D randomizer, never more: areas Ship
  of Harkinian splits differently (Gerudo Fortress, parts of Jabu-Jabu),
  enemy randomizer helpers and Master Quest.
- Ledge Clip and the Forest boss key skip alone give clearly fewer
  locations than in 3D, for the same reason.
- The glitches were not tried in the game; the player performs them.
